The question is whether Tekla makes nut and washer parts on a sag rod. Tekla's own model answers it: in R13 (and R12) the
140 tie rods ROD12 ("Tie Rod THREAD100MM") are plain members with no connection, bolt, nut or washer, Package_2's BOLT-LIST
has no M12 nut on them, and the purlins are drilled where the rods pass. No component dialog value is involved, so no Tekla
video was searched. No Gemini model was used.
