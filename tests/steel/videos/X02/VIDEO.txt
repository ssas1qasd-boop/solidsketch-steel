X01 / X02: no video read.
X01 (built-ups as three plates, Tekla's fittings) and X02 (column tops, cap plates) are not a Tekla component with its own
dialog: the built-ups' plates and the cap plates in R12 were modelled as parts, and their shapes come from Tekla's fittings and
cuts. Tekla's End plate (144) help (support.tekla.com, conn_144_help: "Column end plate connection", the Stiffeners tab) was
read; it makes no plate on top of the column. Tekla has separate cap plate components (e.g. Cap plate detail, Bolted cap
plate (27)), but R12's IFC lists none of them on these 30 plates, so the Column cap plate is a SolidSketch component. Every number comes from
R12 (each web / flange plate, each column top and each of the 30 cap plates measured part by part) and the Tekla package
(assembly drawings R3011, C2017, A2). No Gemini model was used.
