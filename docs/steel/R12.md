# R12: the reference model built from Tekla's IFC

Status: **step 1 of the owner's order of 2026-11-25. This is waiting for the owner's OK.** R11 stays the reference until then, and R10 and R11 are unchanged.

## What R12 is

`tests/steel/models/Frame_28x53m_R12.ss` is built by `tools/golden/r12_build.py` from Tekla's own IFC export, `tests/steel/tekla/Frame_28x53m_tekla_v2.ifc`. The export came from Tekla Structures 2025 SP6 as IFC2X3 Coordination View 2.0 with surface geometry, assemblies, bolts and welds switched on.

The IFC is read by our own reader, `tools/golden/ifc_read.py`. No IFC library is used, because the usual ones are LGPL.

### Parts

There are 4,945 steel, grout and grating parts, plus 176 concrete bodies.

- **Geometry.** Each part keeps Tekla's exact faceted solid. Tekla's bolt holes are drilled into it, using the hole solid of each bolt, and only into the part that surrounds that hole piece. Mesh positions are stored in float32, as the web build stores them.
- **Data from the IFC.** Name, profile, material, class and Tekla's own weight (`st.tkW`) and length (`st.tkL`) all come from the IFC.
- **Plates.** PL and FLT profiles become plates. `pts3` is the largest face, moved to the mid-plane.
- **Members.** All other profiles become members. Their fields (`p1`, `p2`, `rot`, `rAng`, `plane`, `depth`) are solved per member, then proven by rebuilding every member in web v69 (`tools/golden/v69_build.js`):
  - 4,358 of 4,945 parts rebuild within 0.1 mm of Tekla's solid in v69, measured on the section box in Tekla's own frame.
  - The other 587 have features one v69 member or plate cannot hold:
    - hooked anchor rods (192 ROD20, 16 ROD16);
    - skew-cut flange ends (1.8–2.7 mm on the length);
    - stair treads (`THREAD`);
    - flange stiffeners with chamfers.

    Their solids are still Tekla's exact meshes.
- **Assembly.** `st.amark` comes from the IFC assembly. The main part (`st.asm` on the other parts) is the heaviest part of the assembly; on equal weight it is the part named like the assembly. This gives the same main part as R11 on all 4,945 parts.
- **Part mark.** `st.pos` is not in the IFC. R11's mark is kept wherever it agrees with Tekla's Part List (4,941 parts). The other 4 parts get the Part List row that fits.

### Bolts

There are 1,694 groups.

- **Real bolts: 1,288 groups.** Each is a TKBOLT connection plus a bolts body with Tekla's own bolt, washer and nut solid.
  - `pts` is the head's underside, `ax` points toward the nut, and `grip` is Tekla's own (head to washer).
  - Size, length and grade come from Tekla.
  - The parts joined are the parts the hole pieces go through: 2 parts for every real group.
- **Holes only: 406 groups.** These are Tekla bolt groups with no bolt solid (`q.holesOnly`):
  - the 280 M10 groups;
  - anchor-hole groups M20 L4 and L20;
  - M16 L10, L12 and L16.

### Welds

All 6,043 Tekla welds are kept in `st.tkWelds` of the secondary part. Each record holds:
- the main part;
- shop or site;
- around;
- length;
- size and type above and below the line;
- the weld box.

v69 keeps a part's `st` when it opens and saves a file, but drops unknown document fields, so the welds live on the part.

### Checks that R12 opens and saves

- **Web v69.** v69 opens R12 with no page errors (`tools/golden/r12_totals.js`).
- **App.** The app's codec opens R12 and saves it back byte-identical (`SsCodecTest.r12LoadsAndSavesByteIdentical`).

### Weight

The IFC part weights sum to 63,705.82 kg, which is 5.88 kg below the Part List total. The likely cause is that the IFC rounds each part's weight to 0.01 kg, over 4,945 parts; this is not proven.

v69's own weight rules on R12 give 63,859.0 kg, 147 kg above Tekla:
- **SHS100*3:** +190 kg. These parts are not weighed from the catalog (`catalog: false`), so v69 uses the solid, and Tekla exports the SHS with square corners.
- **Anchor rods:** −33 kg.
- **CHS and PD parts:** a few kg.

This is a v69 weight-rule question for step 2, not an R12 data error. `st.tkW` holds Tekla's weight of every part.

## Against R11, part by part

Full tables are in `docs/steel/R12-COMPARE.md`.

### Summary

- 1,507 of 4,945 parts are the same: box within 0.1 mm and the same solid.
- R11 has the same assembly mark as R12 on all parts, and the same main part on all parts.

### C04 purlins and cleats

All 220 purlins have the same box but are **turned 180° about their axis**: R11's C has its web on the other side, 220 of 220.

Because of that, all 264 cleats and 264 stiffeners in R11 sit on the wrong side of the web:
- Tekla's cleat is at s = +30…+90 from the purlin axis, on the web's back;
- R11's cleat is at −90…−30;
- the worst box difference is 120 mm.

The 1,056 M12 4.6 L30 bolts lie on R11's bolt lines, so the holes are the same. Their head point is 72 mm further along, on the other side of the web.

R11 fix 1 (cleat against the web, bolt length 30) is right in kind. Its side came from R10's purlins, which are turned.

### Stairs

- **Brackets:** all 46 are the same as R11, so R11 fix 2 is confirmed by the IFC. R10's brackets differ by their whole volume.
- **Stringers:** 8 have the same box but a different solid, and 4 differ by 32.5 mm.
  - Tekla's UPN240 section (tapered flanges) is not the v69 / R10 one.
  - The stringer ends are cut differently.

### Node gussets

1,053 of 1,206 gusset plates are moved or resized, by up to 286 mm. All 456 connection plates are the same.

The R10 doubts were real; Tekla's positions are in R12.

### The 280 M10 groups

In Tekla these are 'BOLTM10', standard 6.8, length 2, with **no bolt solid**. Each is a hole in one purlin's web, i.e. holes only.

R11 has them as 280 M10 8.8 L80 bolt bodies, so R11's bolts do not exist in Tekla.

### Other bolts

R11's lengths are the importer's (for example M16 L56 ×1,840), not Tekla's (M16 L50 ×1,952). R12 has Tekla's lengths.

### Other parts that moved in R11

- Built-up members: webs and flanges (up to 845 mm on webs);
- bracing (HZ, VL, strut, up to 381 mm);
- anchor rods (110 mm);
- grout (150 mm);
- cap plates (156 mm);
- flange stiffeners (FS / NS, 148 mm);
- haunch plates (43 mm);
- stair plates (112 mm);
- treads (290 mm).

## Rule B on R12

`tools/golden/geom_checks.py` runs on the whole model. Table: `docs/steel/GEOM-CHECKS-R12.md`.

- **FLOATING:** 0 failures.
- **CONTACT:** no designed pairs in an import.
- **OVERLAP and BOLTS:** the groups below. Each group's image is `docs/steel/parity/r12/R12_<group>.png`: the app on the emulator, orthographic, next to Tekla's assembly drawing.

The rule fails on any overlap above 1 mm³. Each overlap's mean depth (2 × volume / surface area of the overlap) is measured too, so a real clash can be told from a face that is shared within storage precision.
