## Reading of the diff

- **New webs:** 3 FLT8*300 (Tekla 103.89 kg each), mark 4518, assembly EB4007.
- **New bolt groups:** 6 (M16 8.8 L45, 48 bolts), each joins a new web to a column shear plate FLT10*150.
- **Shear plates:** 6 column shear plates FLT10*150 (assemblies C2017, C2021, C2022, C2024) now have the 8 holes of those bolts (R12: no holes). Their marks are now 4514, 4515 (R12: 4506).

The webs join the same assemblies. Every R13 assembly holds exactly the parts of its drawing's material list (table below).
- **Welds:** 6 new shop welds, web to flange (st.tkWelds on the flanges).
- **Everything else is unchanged:** 5115 of 5121 paired parts have the same solid, and 1694 of 1694 R12 bolt groups have the same bolts, grip, axis and parts. No R12 part or bolt group is missing in R13.
- Part and assembly marks: Tekla renumbered; R13's marks are Package_2's (see `docs/steel/R13.md`).
