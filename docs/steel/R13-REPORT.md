## How R13 was rebuilt in v69 (the inputs)

- Grid: `x 0 13195 13195 13195 13195`, `y 0 5570 5570 5570 5570 5570`, `z 0 7500 1500` (R13's own, Tekla syntax).
- **Portal frame application: not used.** v69's portal frame (v33, with v42 girts / gables and v43 crane) makes one span at x = 0 (two columns, two rafters per frame) and replaces the parts of an earlier run, so it cannot make R13's four spans on five column lines, its built-up BI220 / BI320 columns, the ridge monitor or the mezzanine. ## Not verified / unsure

- The built-up equivalents are derived (section centre line, square ends at the outermost plate extent on each plate's centre line); Tekla's own member reference line and end cuts are not in R13, so they are compared as solids, not as Tekla inputs.
- R13 data oddities kept as they are: the 16 gable-column webs sit 28.05 mm off the flange centre line, one stair-post flange 12 mm off the web, grout plates up to 130 mm off the base plate centre, and 3 mezzanine girder assemblies have flanges but no web.
- Component settings are taken from R13 where v69 has an option; positions along members (cleats, tongue plates, shear tabs) follow v69's rules, so many detail parts land NEAR or ROUGH rather than MATCHED.
- The app side is not part of this report: it replays `rebuild_inputs.json` through the ported applications later.
