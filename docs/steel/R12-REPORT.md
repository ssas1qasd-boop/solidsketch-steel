## Not verified / unsure

- The built-up equivalents are derived (section centre line, square ends at the outermost plate extent on each plate's centre line); Tekla's own member reference line and end cuts are not in R12, so they are compared as solids, not as Tekla inputs.
- R12 data oddities kept as they are: the 16 gable-column webs sit 28.05 mm off the flange centre line, one stair-post flange 12 mm off the web, grout plates up to 130 mm off the base plate centre, and 3 mezzanine girder assemblies have flanges but no web.
- Component settings are taken from R12 where v69 has an option; positions along members (cleats, tongue plates, shear tabs) follow v69's rules, so many detail parts land NEAR or ROUGH rather than MATCHED.
- The app side is not part of this report: it replays `rebuild_inputs.json` through the ported applications later.
